# Typography Palette — per-creator font pairings

**Read BEFORE writing `<job_dir>/design_brief.md`.** Captions and overlay typography are the single biggest visual lever after avatar likeness. Default Inter-uppercase-with-blue-highlight is competent and forgettable. Pick a palette that matches the **creator's brand register**, not the platform default.

A palette = **body sans + emphasis serif (or handwritten) + numeric/stat display + casing rule + color story**.

---

## How to pick

Map the avatar's register (from `vision.md → position` + `energy_register`) to a palette:

| Avatar register | Palette |
|---|---|
| Etiquette / luxury / editorial expert | **A. Editorial Italic** |
| Modern tech / SaaS / consultant | **B. Modern-Tech** |
| Hype / DTC / sports / energy | **C. Hype Display** |
| Wellness / coaching / heart-led / mindfulness | **D. Coaching Warm** |
| Legal / finance / institutional authority | **E. Authority Serif** |
| Solo creator / handwritten / personal / "from me to you" | **F. High-Touch Personal** |
| Magazine / culture / lifestyle / fashion | **G. Magazine** |
| Minimal / brutalist / no-bullshit founder | **H. Clean Brutalist** |

Don't mix-and-match across palettes. Pick one and let it cascade into captions, overlay headers, stat cards, intro cards, and SFX vocabulary.

## Palettes

### A. Editorial Italic — *Etiquette / Luxury / Editorial Expert*

> The Protocol School of Texas. Aesop. The Wing. New Yorker columnist.

- **Body / captions:** Inter 800 — tight, modern, neutral
- **Emphasis (italic-marked words):** Playfair Display Italic 700 at 1.18× the body size, mixed-case (NOT uppercase)
- **Overlay headers / pills:** Inter 800 uppercase, tight letter-spacing
- **Numeric / stat display:** Inter 900 at very large size, tight letter-spacing, optional drop shadow
- **Casing:** body UPPERCASE, italic-emphasis Mixed Case
- **Color story:** cream / deep navy / warm rust accent / soft grey body
- **Caption color:** white with charcoal-grey outline (or no outline + heavy textShadow)
- **Active-word color:** same as inactive (NO color highlight)

Brands this fits: Diane Gottsman, Aesop, Cire Trudon, Maison Margiela, business-etiquette experts.

---

### B. Modern-Tech — *SaaS / Consultant / B2B*

> Linear. Stripe. Tony Fadell. Tobi Lütke.

- **Body / captions:** Inter 700 medium-tracked, lowercase or sentence case
- **Emphasis:** Inter 900 at 1.1× size + accent color
- **Overlay headers:** Inter 800 uppercase, tighter tracking
- **Numeric / stat display:** Inter 900 — disciplined, minimal
- **Casing:** body sentence-case, emphasis bold (no italic)
- **Color story:** off-black on white / electric accent (lime / electric blue / hot pink) / paper
- **Caption color:** white with thin black outline
- **Active-word color:** bright accent (lime #B6F500, blue #0066FF, or hot pink #FF1F8F)

Brands this fits: Linear, Vercel, Stripe, dev influencers, B2B SaaS founders.

---

### C. Hype Display — *DTC / Sports / High-Energy*

> MrBeast. Alex Hormozi. Andrew Tate (operator-style content). DraftKings.

- **Body / captions:** Bebas Neue 400 OR Anton 400, condensed
- **Emphasis:** Permanent Marker (handwritten chaos) OR larger Bebas at 1.3×
- **Overlay headers:** Anton OR Druk Wide (use Oswald 700 as Druk stand-in)
- **Numeric / stat display:** Bebas Neue at huge sizes, optional rotation
- **Casing:** ALL UPPERCASE, every overlay
- **Color story:** saturated red / black / yellow / electric / no whites
- **Caption color:** bright yellow #FFD400 or white with bold red outline
- **Active-word color:** electric red or black-on-yellow flip

Brands this fits: fitness creators, DTC supplements, viral hooks, "kinetic typography" feed-stoppers.

---

### D. Coaching Warm — *Wellness / Coaching / Heart-led*

> Jay Shetty. Vex King. Maya Angelou quote cards. Co-Star astrology.

- **Body / captions:** Manrope 700 (rounder than Inter, friendlier)
- **Emphasis:** Cormorant Garamond Italic 600 at 1.2× — long-form intimate emphasis
- **Overlay headers:** Cormorant Garamond 500 (sentence case, never uppercase)
- **Numeric / stat display:** Cormorant 600 (don't dominate; numbers serve the story)
- **Casing:** sentence case across the board
- **Color story:** sage / terracotta / cream / dusty rose / warm taupe
- **Caption color:** warm cream #F5EFE3 with soft textShadow
- **Active-word color:** terracotta #C2552C OR sage #6B8E5A

Brands this fits: life coaches, mindfulness practitioners, manifestation creators, soft-skill keynoters.

---

### E. Authority Serif — *Legal / Finance / Institutional*

> Cravath. Goldman. NYT op-ed. Patrick Boyle.

- **Body / captions:** Inter 700 (sober, no display nonsense)
- **Emphasis:** EB Garamond 500 Italic at 1.15× (the "newspaper" feel)
- **Overlay headers:** EB Garamond 600 (sentence case) for cards, Inter 800 uppercase for pills
- **Numeric / stat display:** EB Garamond 500 OR Inter 900 depending on context
- **Casing:** body sentence-case captions, uppercase ONLY on warning pills
- **Color story:** deep navy / off-white / oxblood / no decoration
- **Caption color:** white with hairline black outline
- **Active-word color:** deep oxblood #5A1A1A

Brands this fits: attorneys, financial advisors, tax/estate pros, public-policy commentators.

---

### F. High-Touch Personal — *Solo Creator / Handwritten / "From Me to You"*

> Glennon Doyle. Mari Andrew. Adam J. Kurtz. Morgan Harper Nichols.

- **Body / captions:** Manrope 600 OR Inter 600 (clean foundation)
- **Emphasis:** **Caveat 700 (handwritten) at 1.25×** — the signature "human in the room" treatment
- **Overlay headers:** Caveat 700 OR Permanent Marker (rougher, more masculine)
- **Numeric / stat display:** Caveat OR DM Serif Display (when handwritten feels too soft)
- **Casing:** sentence case body, handwritten emphasis stays as written (mixed case)
- **Color story:** soft pastels / off-black / muted accents / hand-mixed-feeling
- **Caption color:** off-black #1A1A1A on cream OR white on warm grey
- **Active-word color:** muted brand accent (no bright contrasts)

Brands this fits: indie authors, illustrators, intuitive coaches, journal/notebook brands, personal-essay creators. The handwritten font signals *"I made this for you specifically"* — never use for institutional content.

**Variants within F:**
- Friendly / warm → Caveat
- Rougher / bolder → Permanent Marker
- Flourishy / feminine → Dancing Script (use sparingly — easily reads as wedding-invite kitsch)

---

### G. Magazine — *Culture / Fashion / Lifestyle*

> Vogue. Harper's Bazaar. Cabana magazine. Town & Country.

- **Body / captions:** Manrope 600 (clean sans foundation)
- **Emphasis:** Bodoni Moda 700 Italic at 1.2× — high-contrast didone, dramatic
- **Overlay headers:** Bodoni Moda 800 uppercase (fashion-feature feel)
- **Numeric / stat display:** Bodoni Moda 900 with extreme letter-spacing
- **Casing:** UPPERCASE captions + emphasis, Mixed-case headers
- **Color story:** stark black / pure white / single hero color (chartreuse / blood red / electric blue)
- **Caption color:** pure white with no outline (rely on contrast against the avatar)
- **Active-word color:** hero color flash

Brands this fits: fashion creators, beauty editors, lifestyle/aesthetic accounts, art-world commentators.

---

### H. Clean Brutalist — *Minimal / Founder / No-Bullshit*

> Naval Ravikant. Sam Altman. Paul Graham essay layout. Brutalist web design.

- **Body / captions:** Inter 400 (lighter weight than every other palette)
- **Emphasis:** Inter 800 (just heavier, no italic, no color)
- **Overlay headers:** Inter 800 lowercase tight-tracked
- **Numeric / stat display:** Inter 900 enormous, naked
- **Casing:** lowercase across the board (radical for short-form)
- **Color story:** pure black on pure white / single accent / nothing decorative
- **Caption color:** white with black outline OR black with white outline (depending on background)
- **Active-word color:** same as inactive

Brands this fits: solo VC partners, indie tech founders, philosophy creators, "thinking-out-loud" style accounts.

## Handwritten font usage rules

Handwritten fonts (Caveat, Permanent Marker, Dancing Script) are the most polarizing and easiest-to-misuse choice. Rules:

1. **Use sparingly.** Maximum 1-2 word emphasis per beat. Whole sentences in handwritten font read as a fake quote card from 2014 Pinterest.
2. **Pair with a disciplined sans body.** Caveat + Inter is bulletproof. Caveat + Bebas Neue is chaotic.
3. **Size up the handwritten relative to body** (1.2-1.4×) — small handwritten reads as a Word-document scribble.
4. **Color-match the wardrobe / room palette.** Handwritten in #FFFFFF on a busy background reads sloppy; handwritten in a warm cream that picks up the room tones reads on-brand.
5. **Casing follows the font's natural form.** Caveat is designed for mixed-case casual; Permanent Marker is designed for ALL-CAPS shout-marker.

**When handwritten is wrong:**
- Institutional / corporate / legal / financial content
- Hype / DTC / sports (use Permanent Marker only, NOT Caveat)
- High-stakes B2B (handwritten signals informality — wrong for "$50k consulting package")

## Color story rules

Every palette has 3-5 colors max. Pick ONE accent for active-word highlights (or skip highlights entirely — Editorial / High-Touch / Brutalist palettes don't highlight at all). Match overlay card backgrounds to the avatar's wardrobe + setting tones — the goal is **visual cohesion between the avatar's room and the typography**, not branded screen takeovers that feel pasted on.

If you can't articulate the 3-color palette in one sentence, the design brief isn't done.

---

## Decision aid — when in doubt

Ask: **"What would a reader of [the avatar's natural publication] expect to see?"**

- Etiquette expert → New Yorker, Vogue → Editorial Italic OR Magazine
- Career coach → HBR → Modern-Tech OR Authority Serif
- Bankruptcy attorney → ABA Journal → Authority Serif
- Astrologer → Co-Star app, Cosmopolitan → Coaching Warm OR Magazine
- Tech founder talking strategy → Stripe blog → Modern-Tech OR Clean Brutalist
- Mindfulness coach → Calm app — Coaching Warm
- Etiquette + irreverent younger demo → Editorial Italic with Magazine accents
