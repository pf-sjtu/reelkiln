# Minho — Ideal Viewer Persona (45-min lesson, full transcript)

Pedagogical source. Different from the 370-video observational sample — this
is a working creator (Minho, ~500k IG followers in two years on his personal
brand) explicitly teaching the upstream framework that produces the hooks
and scripts the rest of `viralizer_research/` reverse-engineers.

The most important takeaways for our pipeline (these drive structural changes
in vision.md and viralizer.md):

1. **Average watch time on the first ~1,000 viewers determines distribution.**
   Algorithm pushes high-AWT videos exponentially, not linearly. So every
   second of the first 10s has to fight for retention.

2. **Perceived value in 5-10 seconds.** Viewer's question: "is this for me?
   will it pay off?" — answered (consciously or not) within 5 seconds.

3. **Ideal Viewer Persona (IVP) — one named person, not a demographic.**
   Not "professionals 25-40." A *specific* person at a *specific* life-stage
   in a *specific* current emotional moment. Minho's worked example:
   *"20-year-old college student who just got rejected from half their
   internships and is panicking that AI is going to take the rest."*

4. **Core human drives sit underneath every surface topic.** Survival, money,
   reproduction, status. The viral hook fires at the deeper drive and uses
   the surface tactic as the mechanism. Yerba mate (surface) → "magic potion
   I drink to run my business and post a video every day" (productivity /
   money / status drive).

5. **AGIQ register.** Read the hook as a tired person on the toilet. Hooks
   work via hyper-simplicity, not technical depth. *"120mg caffeine, here's
   how you match the L-theanine ratio from 120 to 240"* = scrolled. Same
   topic, hooked at the deeper drive, in plain language = lands.

6. **Find the viewer in their current emotional state, then PULL them
   through a journey.** The premed example: hook = *"rejected from every
   med school"* (where they ARE) → reveal = *"academic comeback"* (where
   they're going). The video bridges from current state to dream state.

7. **Position: mentor (older/wiser) OR friend (same boat).** Both work,
   produce different scripts. (Our pipeline: mentor / credible-witness only
   for now, since the avatar is generated and lacks an authentic peer
   history.)

8. **Lore = positioning, not adjectives.** Nick Saraj's AI content lands
   harder than other AI channels because his positioning is "practical AI
   for making money in a job market that's eating people alive" — that's the
   lore. Adjectives like "meticulous" carry positioning when there's a
   positioning to carry; without it they're decoration.

9. **Emotion before information.** *"Holy shit Open Claw just did my entire
   semester's worth of studying in 2 minutes"* beats *"Here's how to use
   Open Claw with the Anthropic API."* Same content, different layer.

10. **Hyper-niche is GOOD.** A line that hits hard for ONE specific person
    is the goal. The "could appear in any coach's video" test from the
    skill is wrong if "any coach" means "any audience" — niche-specificity
    is the win. The right test: does this land HARDER for our IVP than for
    a generic viewer?
