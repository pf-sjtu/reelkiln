# Niche calibration — what each of our 7 niches rewards

Different niches reward different defaults. After you've drafted a script and
applied the universal punch-up rules in `agent/skills/viralizer.md`, tune
the dial for the niche of the reference outlier.

This was previously §10 of viralizer.md; moved here to keep the skill
operating-doctrine focused, with this file as the lookup reference.

---

## business-life-and-marriage-coaches

- **Lean toward:** verbatim dialogue scripts (negotiation, interview, email
  rewrites), insider-confession framing, recruiter-POV ("here's what we
  actually look at"), "drop these phrases" imperatives.
- **Avoid:** abstract self-help. *"Be intentional"* dies; *"Before you open
  Slack, ask: is this on-purpose for me today?"* lives.
- **Champions:** advicewitherin (skit), jobdoctortessa ($85k for $130k),
  greglangstaff (resume transformation), theintrovertedrecruiter (listicle
  structure), courtney..johnson (audited 500 LinkedIn posts).

## fitness-parenting-and-financial-coaches

- **Lean toward:** time-cost shock numbers, mechanism + prescription pattern
  (*"reptilian brain → prefrontal offline → ask a random question"*),
  verbatim scripts for difficult conversations (boundaries with relatives),
  protein/dosage formulas, kid-quiz authority flexes.
- **Avoid:** abstract advice without dosage. *"Eat more protein"* dies; *"1g
  per lb of goal body weight, divided across 3-4 meals"* lives.
- **Champions:** rorydouglasofficial (kid-quiz), moneycoachvince (sponsored
  listicle), walkers.therapy (pattern-interrupt), ab.hightower (boundary
  scripts), nicktalksmoney (skit format).

## real-estate-and-property

- **Lean toward:** price-anchored house tours with a comment-question close,
  cautionary-tale flexes ("$137k commission and I pissed it away"),
  flip-deal numbers exposition (bought-for / all-in / sold-for / profit),
  series-cliffhanger setups.
- **Avoid:** generic market commentary. *"Real estate is a great asset"*
  dies; *"$960k purchase, $250k all-in, $1M profit on this one"* lives.
- **Champions:** robneidlinger (sales skit), glenndabaker (cautionary tale),
  sanchezrealty (price-tour), section8karim (extreme outcome), garrettbrownre (DIY).

## professional-and-legal-services

- **Lean toward:** "X things I would NEVER do as a [credential]" listicles,
  "things I tell my kids as a [defender]", one-rule scary facts ("never put
  up a 'beware of dog' sign"), tax-savings receipt drops, parenting
  truth-bombs.
- **Avoid:** lawyer-disclaimer language ("this is not legal advice but…")
  unless said with clear personality. Audience expects opinion.
- **Champions:** mpestateplanninguk ("6 things I'd never do"), thepaynelawyer
  (beware-of-dog rule), lawmotherco (skit walkthrough), attorneyadamashby
  (kid felony warning), carlottathompsonfinance (tax receipt drop).

## b2b-consultants-and-agency-owners

- **Lean toward:** "Nobody knows this about [tool]" secret-hack reveals,
  "marketers are going to hate me for this" anti-establishment positioning,
  case-study expose ("I'm about to expose his entire strategy"),
  worst-client confession stories, AI-tool live demos.
- **Avoid:** thought-leader-jargon. *"Drive synergistic outcomes"* dies;
  *"Go to Google Search Console, click here, paste this regex"* lives.
- **Champions:** heytonyagency (jab-jab-right-hook skit), build_in_public
  (Google secret), eddiecumberbatch (strategy expose), zanderfryer (worst
  client), adamcbenjamin (sponsored LinkedIn ads).

## ecommerce-and-founders

- **Lean toward:** TikTok-shop affiliate testimonials with a disclaimer-
  endorsement combo (*"I don't care if y'all don't buy this, but I'm so
  grateful I found it"*), BTS warehouse-vlogs with cliffhanger beats,
  origin-story flexes, "5 years ago I was…" → multimillionaire arcs.
- **Avoid:** generic product description language. Affiliate testimonials
  are a SHOW format — demonstrate the BEFORE-state on camera.
- **Champions:** zayzayexpress (dirty-secret affiliate), ailiabottle (BTS
  warehouse), willchangelives (Amazon-FBA origin), shopbyjake (GLP affiliate).

## doctors-medical-and-clinics

- **Lean toward:** horror-medical narratives with quoted dialogue and
  X%-fatal-but-Y%-preventable closes; visual-comparison surgical reveals
  (normal vs post-op); "you're not crazy, this has a name" symptom-naming;
  one-rule scary facts ("kissing somebody with a beard can cause an
  infection").
- **Avoid:** clinical jargon without translation. Medical content wins on
  mechanism explained in non-doctor language.
- **Caution:** higher misinformation risk; cite mechanisms verifiably; never
  imply diagnosis.
- **Champions:** doctormyro (brain amoeba), doctorsooj (post-tonsillectomy
  reveal), drabby6 (sponsored derm), josceljames (nurse rant).
