Sample skews to career/job-search and life-coaching content. Top performers in raw views are skit-format career coaches (advicewitherin, jobdoctortessa, greglangstaff). Top by outlier-ratio are creators with tiny baselines who hit one breakout (sharonkgillenwater 10357x, courtney..johnson 5653x, theintrovertedrecruiter 3319x).

## Hook archetypes that landed

1. **"One of the trickiest" + scripted skit**
   > "How to answer one of the trickiest job interview questions: what are your salary expectations?" (advicewitherin, 23M views)
   Then immediately plays the WRONG answers (cringe) → "Stop, don't answer this directly" → models the RIGHT answer verbatim.

2. **Audited-N + contrarian discovery**
   > "as a social media consultant I have audited over 500 high performing LinkedIn posts and what I discovered completely defies the conventional wisdom of this platform" (courtney..johnson, 14M views, 5653x)

3. **Insider-confession with money-on-the-line**
   > "I just offered a candidate $85,000 for a job that had a budget of $130,000. I offered her that because it's what she asked for and I personally don't have the bandwidth to give lessons on salary negotiation." (jobdoctortessa, 9.4M)

4. **Transformation reveal** (live-edit format)
   > "let's turn this 1 out of 10 resume into 10 out of 10 resume" (greglangstaff, 12.7M)
   Format: real-time narrated edit ("first we have a master's, let's add that. Next we don't need the street address, bye bye"). Each edit is a micro-payoff.

5. **"They didn't want you to read this"**
   > "LinkedIn did not intend for job seekers to read this article, but you've got to. It's written by their Chief Product Officer..." (selfmademillennial, 1.6M)

6. **Drop-the-phrase imperative**
   > "Remove 'hope you're doing well' from your emails if you want to grow in your career." (thecareerdiet, 1.6M)
   > "How often do you say 'I just wanted'... eliminate both 'just' and 'wanted'." (thecareerdiet, 374k)

7. **Closed-door / contrarian rule against the consensus**
   > "Need to have a closed door policy when it comes to HR. Do not talk to them about any of your problems." (strategytips, 1.5M)

8. **Identity-target conditional**
   > "if your teenager is lost and has no career direction, it's always grumpy and depressed, you don't know how to help them. Listen to this." (miriam_career, 1.4M)

9. **Authority + frustration vent ("done sugarcoating")**
   > "lightning round of corporate truths that no one is talking about... I'm not here to spread fear... after years of being diplomatic I'm done sugarcoating" (elite.recruiter, 3.5M)

10. **Pull-not-push reframe** (drama-free, useful imperative)
    > "How do you answer 'why do you want to leave your current job'? You should frame it as a pull instead of a push." (selfmademillennial, 1.3M)

11. **Senior-rule reveal**
    > "If you're the most senior person in your zoom meeting, you should not be presenting." (strategytips, 1.3M)

12. **Aphorism / paradox opener**
    > "Nobody changes until they change their energy." (mindpower86, 3M)
    > "the unhappiest people I've met in my life all have the exact same traits. they are extremely ambitious, but lazy as fuck." (guillaumemoubeche, 2.7M)

13. **Story teaser ("I just got kicked out")**
    > "So I just got kicked out of my hotel, and the authorities were called in Chautauqua, New York. So I'm gonna tell you what happened because it is hilarious." (sharonkgillenwater, 8.5M, 559s long-form story)

14. **Identity-pulled hot-take**
    > "If I was a woman in this lifetime, which I'm not, but if I was, I would very much pay attention to the relationship the guy I was interested in had with his mom." (sabastianenges, 3M)

15. **Pure diagnostic listicle ("3 signs that")**
    > "These are 3 signs that you might have high functioning anxiety." (robdial, 6.1M)

16. **Coaching-as-intervention** (apply expertise to a specific named person/case)
    > "if I were Kaisenad's personal speaking coach, here are four more strategies I would give him" (npcfaizan, 1.9M)

## Re-hook moves (every 3-5 sec)

- **"Stop, don't do that. Try this."** — the most powerful re-hook in coach skits. Hard pivot mid-scene, creates a structural beat.
- **"Stick around to the end because I'll also share..."** — explicit promise of bonus payoff.
- **"Are you paying attention?"** — direct retention-pump (selfmademillennial uses this verbatim).
- **"But here's where this becomes interesting."** (sharon's story — flags the climb).
- **"Mistake number 1... Mistake number 2..."** — counter-listicle nested inside a listicle.
- **"Before I give you the final pro tip, let's talk about what NOT to say."** — withholds the headline; teases the contrast.
- **"Let me tell you what happened."** (sharon, narrative drum-beat).
- **"Notice the difference."** / **"See the difference?"** (after teaching a fix — invites the AHA).
- **"Let's put that into practice."** — pivot from theory to script (theintrovertedrecruiter).

## Story arc / payoff shapes

- **Skit (WRONG → STOP → RIGHT)** — most repeatable, highest-views in this niche. Wrong answers played for cringe (Um, I don't know, don't care...). Hard cut "Stop, don't answer this directly" is the structural breakpoint. Right answers modeled with full verbatim dialogue. Closes "You got this. Follow me for more." The PAYOFF is the viewer mentally rehearsing the right answer — cathartic.
- **Listicle (Hook → 3-7 numbered → mistakes-to-avoid → CTA)** — theintrovertedrecruiter and elite.recruiter both run 200s+ versions. Pacing: ~20-25s per item, with a re-hook before items 1 and 5. Final beat: "if you do that, you'll never struggle with this again."
- **Story-rant** (sharonkgillenwater) — 9-min monologue. Builds via accumulated grievances, named antagonists (Bitsy/Bunny/Betsy/Helen), repeated sensory specifics ("laminated sign", "$400 a night", "Eastern European masseuse"). Climax: confrontation. Coda: lessons + invitation to follow-up content. Payoff = righteous-anger relief plus the comedic detail.
- **Diagnostic ("3 signs that...")** — payoff is the viewer self-identifying. Closes with "comment below if you can relate" — converting the recognition to engagement.
- **Reveal walkthrough** ("LinkedIn didn't intend...") — payoff is feeling like an insider. Each fact is a small payoff, accumulating to "actionable list of moves to make right now."
- **Transformation reveal** (resume 1→10) — micro-payoff per edit ("much better"). Final reveal is the contrast cut. CTA in description.

## CTA patterns (baked into dialogue)

- **Reassurance + Follow** — "You got this. Follow me for more." (closes the skit emotionally before asking)
- **Comment-as-magnet** — "Comment 'COURSE' below and I'll send you your way totally free" (healthpreneur)
- **Save-this-for-later** — "save this and watch tomorrow morning before you open email"
- **Bookmark imperative** — "you might want to bookmark this for future reference, just saying :)" (deepali — note the smiley)
- **Self-identification + comment** — "if you can relate to any of these 3, put it down below and let's talk about it" (robdial)
- **Resources-in-bio** with directive ("link is in my bio. Don't ever pay for a CV writer.") — strong opinion + free download.
- **Recurring-channel pitch with frustration framing** — "if you want no-nonsense practical career advice from someone who's spent 2 decades actually hiring them, hit subscribe so you don't miss out" (theintrovertedrecruiter — note "no-nonsense" framing as anti-CV-writer-grifter positioning)

## Voice & personality moves

- **Specificity at every line** — "$85k", "$130k", "78%", "100k followers", "65-95K range" — never round abstractions
- **Named techniques they coined** — "coffee badging", "model of picture capture", "pull instead of push", "rapid fire prep", "lightning round of corporate truths"
- **Profanity used as emphasis-marker, not filler** — "ambitious but lazy as fuck", "bitch, I'm paying 400 bucks a night" — earned by emotional context
- **Bracketed asides** — "(because the roles I've held)", "just saying :)" — the parenthetical is part of the rhythm
- **Personal vendetta = retention** — Sharon names "Helen" as the villain; the fight gives the audience someone to root against
- **Reluctant insider tone** — "I personally don't have the bandwidth to give lessons on salary negotiation. Let's go over this again..." Implies "I can't believe I have to say this again"
- **Verbatim scripts** — show, don't tell. Tessa, advicewitherin, juliastarr all give the EXACT words to say
- **Self-positioning via what they're NOT** — "I'm not here to spread fear", "no-nonsense", "done sugarcoating", "we're not gonna mince words"

## Anti-patterns this niche avoids

- "Hi everyone, I'm [name], today we're going to talk about..." — never seen in top performers
- Generic moral lessons without verbatim scripts — capped much lower
- Long author-credentialing intros (AT MOST one mid-content credential drop, like Lee at theintrovertedrecruiter ~30s in)
- "Self-care matters" / abstract feel-goodery without a number, name, or script attached

## Niche-specific tactics

- **Career advice videos lean on PROOF dialogue scripts.** The proof of usefulness is a verbatim sentence the viewer can copy-paste. This is the killer feature of this niche.
- **Recruiter-insider POV** is consistently overpowered ("I'm a recruiter, here's what we actually look at"). Authority + insider-pique = irresistible.
- **HR / corporate "rules" content** — "what your boss won't tell you", "what HR is really for" — taps a low-trust workplace mood.
- **Tessa's "wish I had a do-over"** confessional — vulnerability from a person who's seen the other side carries enormous weight (3.1M for a 22-second talking head).
- **Life-coach abstract ("change your energy")** can hit but is more reliant on creator-aura than script. Mindpower86 has the cadence but the script alone doesn't replicate.
