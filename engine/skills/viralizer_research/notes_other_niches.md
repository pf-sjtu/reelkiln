## real-estate-and-property (60 videos)

**Distinctive hook archetypes:**

1. **Price-anchored house tour** (the dominant format in this niche)
   > "This is what $130,000 is gonna get you in Texas. Now let's take a look inside." (sanchezrealty, 6.3M)
   Vertical 9:16 walkthrough, room-by-room narration, comedic asides ("don't be looking at my booty"), comment-question close ("Is this worth $130k? Let me know.").

2. **Cautionary-tale flex** (vulnerable money-regret)
   > "Once upon a time, I sold a big-ass house and made $137,000 in commission. And rather than investing, I bought a brand-new Mercedes... that townhouse today is worth $1,000,000. That Mercedes is worth zero, and those clothes don't fit." (glenndabaker, 10.6M)

3. **Skit-as-teach (sales coaching)**
   > "Stop using the F-word in sales. What's the F-word in sales? Let's pretend... Hello, this is Rob. How are you? I am just FINE. — The word's 'fine'." (robneidlinger, 16.1M)
   Teaching-via-roleplay. Two-person reveal.

4. **Flip-deal exposition with raw numbers**
   > "I bought this building for $960,000. My all-in is less than $250,000. I got a bank loan for $720,000. Construction is $1M. I'm gonna sell for $3.3M — over a million-dollar profit on this one project." (thesunilsaxena, 3.4M)

5. **Series opener with cliffhanger collage**
   > "It's officially been one year since I bought this 15-unit apartment building for $1M. I found treasures in the basement, a car crash into my building, fire damage. Follow along — I'm doing a series." (investorgirlbritt)

6. **Government-loophole flex with extreme outcome**
   > "I'm 21 years old, I own 77 Section-8 rentals. I have a Lamborghini Urus directly paid for by the government." (section8karim, 1.8M)

7. **DIY-with-pricetag** (overlaps with home-improvement audience)
   > "Here's how I built a custom 8-foot cowboy pool for under $800." (garrettbrownre, 10.5M)

8. **Long-form economic monologue** (trust-built audience)
   zacharyloft — 9-minute Federal-Reserve / debt-cycle / class-warfare rant. Works on a 1.9M follower base, NOT for cold-start.

9. **Real-estate-bestie product rec**
   > "When I bought my house, the sellers told me they had a golf-ball toilet. I thought it was weird. But that toilet has never backed up. So today I'm buying another golf-ball toilet." (alishacollinsrealestate)
   Calls audience "real estate bestie" — parasocial framing.

**Re-hook moves unique to niche:**
- "Now let's take a look inside / upstairs / at the master..." (room-transition)
- "Now let me know in the comments — would you block your X for this home?"
- "I'm just kidding. You guys can't take a joke." (comedic break)
- "Send this to somebody who doesn't live in Texas" (share-trigger)

**Story arc / payoff:**
- House-tour: room teleport → mini-jokes → shock-price reframe at the end → comment-question
- Cautionary-tale: confession → past-value vs current-value math → "Mercedes is worth zero, clothes don't fit" coda. Payoff = relief + lesson absorbed without lecture
- Flip-deal: bought-for / all-in / sold-for / profit. Numbers = the payoff. Always closes "If you wanna learn how, hit the plus sign."

**CTA patterns unique to niche:**
- "Hit the plus sign to learn how" (TikTok plus = follow/save)
- "DM me to learn how to buy your first section-8 rental"
- "Form in bio. Let's schedule a private tour."

**Champions to re-read:** robneidlinger (sales skit), glenndabaker (cautionary tale), sanchezrealty (price-tour), section8karim (extreme outcome flex), garrettbrownre (DIY).

## professional-and-legal-services (60 videos)

**Distinctive hook archetypes:**

1. **"Things I would NEVER do as a [credentialed expert]"** (the gold pattern)
   > "6 things I would never do as an asset protection specialist. Number one: I would never leave anything to my partner or children in my will." (mpestateplanninguk, 5.1M, 4581x)
   Listicle format with the AUTHORITY framing — "if I, the expert, wouldn't do this, why would you?"

2. **"Things I tell my kids as a [criminal defense attorney]"**
   > "If you are ever with friends and something illegal happens, drugs, fighting, vandalism, improper photos, you leave immediately. Yes, even if it's the group chat. Because in Arizona, presence is participation." (attorneyadamashby)
   Combines "I'm a parent too" relatability + scary-fact authority.

3. **One-rule scary fact** (no listicle — single counter-intuitive imperative)
   > "Never put up a sign that says 'beware of dog.' It will be used as exhibit one if your dog bites someone... Instead, put up a sign that says 'dog on property.'" (thepaynelawyer, 5.7M)

4. **Skit-walkthrough of a legal scenario**
   > "My husband died and I need access to his iCloud account. Did he make you his legacy contact? What's that?..." (lawmotherco, 5.4M) — two-actor playlet

5. **PSA in real-time** (commentary on someone's actual mistake)
   > "PSA, this is exactly what you are NOT supposed to do when ICE comes to your door. What do we say? Do you have a warrant? Slide the warrant under the door." (yunuslaw, 6.3M) — narrating over a viral video, naming wrong moves and right moves

6. **Tax-savings receipt drop**
   > "Client made $569,131. Tax bill was $176,700. She did $69k of [specific deductions]... her tax bill went to $9,167. Then she qualified for credits, putting $500,000 back in her pocket." (carlottathompsonfinance, 2M, 19624x)
   Audacious specific dollar receipts.

7. **Numbered tax-rules listicle**
   > "Here are the top 5 write-offs for new LLC owners. Number one: take your home office deduction with the advanced method..." (karltondennis, 2M)

8. **Marriage / parenting truth-bomb (high-trust voice)**
   > "Date your wife before someone else does. You dated her to marry her. Keep dating her to stay married to her... marriage is not a finish line, it's a starting line." (markdriscoll, 8.9M)

9. **Specific-fact felony warning**
   > "If your kid is in a group text, they're one screenshot away from a felony." (attorneyadamashby)

**Re-hook moves:**
- "And here's what most parents don't realize..."
- "Pro tip: a life insurance trust is free." (mid-list bonus)
- "But if they're still living in the house after they put it into trust..." (the gotcha continuation)

**Story arc / payoff:**
- Listicle "I would never": each item = parallel structure (NEVER X. INSTEAD Y.). Payoff = mental checklist viewer can apply.
- Kid-warning: setup ("good kids in group chat") → consequence (felony, future destroyed) → fix (don't open it, delete) → values close ("be smart, don't be stupid")
- Tax-receipt drop: client setup → starting bill → list of deductions → ending bill. Payoff = the SAVINGS DELTA, said as a number.

**CTAs unique to niche:**
- "Call me if things get complicated." (attorneys — implicit referral)
- "If you have a question, comment below or DM me." 
- "Thanks, Law Mother." (sister-style sign-off — namechecks the brand)

**Champions to re-read:** mpestateplanninguk ("6 things I would never do"), thepaynelawyer ("beware of dog" sign), lawmotherco (iCloud skit), attorneyadamashby (group chat felony), carlottathompsonfinance (tax receipts).

## doctors-medical-and-clinics (38 videos)

**Distinctive hook archetypes:**

1. **Horror-medical-story opener**
   > "Abandoned mansion and a pool, he jumps in. Except it's a perfect breeding ground for brain-eating amoeba. The neurologist came in and told us: '4 days. You have 4 days with your son.'" (doctormyro, 50.6M, 484x)
   Visual-narrative re-enactment with quoted dialogue. Then mechanism. Then "100% preventable."

2. **Visual-comparison surgical reveal**
   > "That looks nasty, but what is it? This is a normal throat, and this is what a normal throat looks like after someone has had their tonsils removed." (doctorsooj, 46.5M)
   Side-by-side reveal → mechanism explanation → red-flag warnings.

3. **"Did you know X can cause Y?"**
   > "Kissing somebody with a beard can cause an infection? Sure can. If not clean, the beard can harbor staph and strep..." (doctormyro, 23.2M)

4. **Defining a symptom most people quietly have**
   > "Some people really can't stand the sound of other people chewing, breathing, clicking a pen — this is called misophonia." (doctorsooj, 15M)
   Naming a private experience → "you're not crazy, this has a name."

5. **Aesthetic procedure diary** (doctor + patient banter, drroshofficial)
   > "I don't feel like I need much, and Botox, I think this lifts ever so slightly more... maybe a little bit in my chin... do you reckon we could just touch upon that?" 
   First-person POV of the patient → casual injector banter → before/after.

6. **Sponsored derm partnership**
   > "Do you know what no one ever talks about? That the scalp is skin... As a dermatologist, I partnered with CeraVe to reinvent the basic hair care routine." (drabby6, 12.4M)

7. **Nurse / professional rant**
   > "Nursing is the most toxic profession of all time. One minute you're coding grandma, then you're getting yelled at because someone waited 30 minutes in the ER." (josceljames, 304k, 102x)
   Industry-truth vent. Reels viewers in by validating the rage.

**Re-hook moves:**
- "And THAT is what [scary thing] can do." (after the dramatic story setup)
- "So what should you do?" 
- "Send this to someone who's had [X]" (doctorsooj uses repeatedly — share-trigger)

**Story arc / payoff:**
- Horror-story: vivid setting → ordinary protagonist → diagnosis-shock → mechanism → "X% fatal but Y% preventable" close. Payoff = scared awareness + actionable preventive imperative.
- Misophonia-style "you're not crazy": private discomfort → name → mechanism → fixes. Payoff = relief + share-trigger.
- Nurse rant: laundry-list of horrors → "and you're just expected to go home like everything's okay" → "we are pawns" — payoff is collective recognition.

**CTAs:**
- "Send this to someone who's had [X]"
- "Save this video for later"
- "Comment if this happened to you"

**Niche caution:** Health content has higher misinformation risk. Always verifiable claims with cited mechanisms. Doctormyro's brain-eating-amoeba video isn't viral because of horror — it's viral because the mechanism (Naegleria fowleri swims up the nose) is explained clearly enough that the prevention sticks.

**Champions to re-read:** doctormyro (brain amoeba horror), doctorsooj (post-tonsillectomy reveal), drabby6 (sponsored derm), josceljames (nurse rant).
